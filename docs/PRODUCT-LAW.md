# Product Law — Human Nature Content Engine

## Atomic unit
**Human Insight** is the atomic unit. No content asset, concept, or visual may be generated without a parent insight.

## Ten product laws
1. **Insight-first** — Every piece of content traces to one or more approved Human Insights.
2. **Human truth over motivation** — Prefer uncomfortable recognition over generic uplift.
3. **Conflict is the engine** — Name desire, hidden fear, contradiction, and cost.
4. **Taxonomy is editable truth** — Conflicts live in DB tables, not hardcoded enums forever.
5. **Genome over vibes** — Content carries an explicit genome (conflict, emotion, lens, structure).
6. **Dedup before publish** — Novelty and recognition are scored; duplicates are judged.
7. **Feedback is data** — Approve/reject/edit/favorite/regenerate/publish events are stored.
8. **Idea Vault is the inventory** — Metrics: total, approved, unused, used, exceptional, needs_research, rejected, retired.
9. **Runs are auditable** — Every agent action logs under `RUN-YYYY-MM-DD-NNN`.
10. **Page DNA guides voice** — Each page has topics, voice, visual mix, and format mix.

## Non-goals (Phase 1)
- Full agent autonomy
- Production LLM wiring
- Multi-platform auto-publish
